A FAMILY, NOT A BAG OF ICONS.

Shared chamfers, strong negative space and a central signal motif. Navigation, resources and abilities have different jobs but the same visual ancestry. These paths are original studies, not traced reference-game assets. Short labels and accessible names remain part of the production contract.

SCENES, NOT PAGES

Do not rebuild the universal header/card/footer wrapper. Share spatial rules while allowing each game moment a different composition.
