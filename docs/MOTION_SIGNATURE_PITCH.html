THE FULL MOMENT MAP.

RESOLUTION / 350-500 MS

KNOWLEDGE BECOMES FORCE.

Once a resolution is published, a short signal trace connects the answered plate to the Brawler's committed attack. Two brush-smear poses, a 40-60 ms cosmetic contact hold, then STRIKE. BLOCK gets compression and a shield ripple, not a damage explosion.

Never reveal correctness early. Never freeze the battle engine.
ROSTER / 420-600 MS AFTER SAVE

OWN YOUR ENTRANCE.

A diagonal print-mask reveals the equipped Brawler; their personal sigil and name land on separate beats. The selection becomes a player identity, not a checked radio card.

Inspect is not equip. Locked content never pretends to unlock.
DRAFT / 240-320 MS

STAMP THE DECISION.

Ban scars the crest with one decisive slash. Pick stamps the emblem into the real five-category belt. Three ink chips are enough. Keep category names and the next action available.

Commitment follows valid draft state, not an invented countdown.
RESULT / 600-900 MS

WIN THE COMPOSITION.

VICTORY cuts into a clean winner tableau. Saved XP flows into the actual progress bar; a real level-up receives one number/emblem stamp. Rematch is never hostage to the celebration.

No fake rank-up, extra currency, repeated history reward or unapproved chest change.
